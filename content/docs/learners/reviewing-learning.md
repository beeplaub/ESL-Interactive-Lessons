## Review a completed lesson

After completing a lesson, BrenUp shows a short summary with your score, attempted activities, and question count. Choose **Review** to revisit your saved work or **Retake** when you want new evidence.

## Review quiz attempts

Quiz results are saved for signed-in learners. Your recent attempts show the score, date, and answer feedback. Timed attempts remain separate because the timer is part of the assessment context.

## Understand scores and outcomes

Some course activities are mapped to lesson and course outcomes. Your score contributes to outcome attainment, while **coverage** shows how much of the available evidence you have attempted. A high score on one question does not automatically complete an entire outcome.
