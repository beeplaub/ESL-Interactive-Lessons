## Open your inbox

Select the bell or open **Notifications** from your learner menu. The small notification panel keeps things brief. The inbox holds the full message, its action button, and your message history.

## Follow feedback directly to the work

When a teacher sends writing feedback, open the message and choose its action. BrenUp takes you back to the exact lesson, slide, and activity where the feedback belongs.

## Keep the inbox useful

You can mark a message unread, archive it to trash, or restore it later. Archived messages stay private to your account until they are permanently removed by your own actions or an applicable retention rule.

## Control delivery

Open **Notification settings** inside the inbox to choose in-app, email, browser push, and sound preferences. Some important account and safety messages may still be delivered when required.
