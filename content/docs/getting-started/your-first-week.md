## 1. Find your starting point

Take the **Level Test** if you have not already received a level. It gives BrenUp a useful starting point for course and quiz recommendations. Your result is a guide, not a limit.

## 2. Join a course

Open **Courses**, choose a published course that fits your current level, then enroll. Your course page shows the order of lessons, what is unlocked, and your progress.

## 3. Learn, practise, and return

Lessons separate learning content from interactive practice. BrenUp keeps unfinished, untimed activity work available while you move between slides or tabs in the same signed-in session.

## 4. Check your language profile

Your **Language Profile** turns activity and quiz evidence into a view of skills, learning targets, and course Can-Dos. It grows as you submit more work.

> A small, repeatable routine is more useful than trying to complete everything in a single day.
