## Roles have different responsibilities

**Platform Admins** manage BrenUp-wide policies and content. **School Admins** manage their school workspace, people, and school-owned learning. **Teachers** teach assigned classes and work with their own permitted content. **Learners** see only their assigned or enrolled learning. Guardians see only their linked learner.

## Begin with the right structure

Create or open the organization, add teachers and learners, then create classes. Courses, lessons, and assignments can be organized around those classes without changing the reusable original content.

## Use reports responsibly

Class and course reports show completion, evidence, outcome attainment, and coverage. Use them to plan support, not to reduce a learner to a single score.
