# IPA Chart Word Expansion — Working Master List

## Scope

The monophthong, diphthong, and consonant passes are complete. For each sound card, retain the live chart's current examples, then propose three disyllabic and three trisyllabic examples. Use the same word list for UK and US where a corresponding sound card exists; keep the two IPA transcriptions separate. Accent-specific cards are documented only for the accent where they exist.

The IPA below follows Cambridge Dictionary pronunciation entries. Syllable counts follow the syllabification shown there. Brackets mark the spelling to highlight in the admin form; for consonant cards, that spelling corresponds to the target sound. These are proposals for review, not words added to the live catalog. New entries are added only after checking the Cambridge UK and US entries, syllable division, target sound, spelling highlight, and live catalog for duplicates. For UK-only cards, the opposite-accent IPA is comparison only, not an additional sound-card batch. Audio is out of scope.
